# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Loop's contributor community to be as welcoming as we want the app itself to be for students. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews and discussions
- Assume good faith; critique the code, not the person
- Welcome newcomers and questions — everyone was a first-time contributor once

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers directly. Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this code.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
